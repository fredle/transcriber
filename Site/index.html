without lifting a finger.

Teeline sits in your tray and transcribes Microsoft Teams calls in real time, starting and stopping itself with the meeting. Afterwards, search the transcript, see who joined and left, and ask questions about what was actually said.

Updates itself

Your meetings, your account

Teeline is a small tray app, not a bot that joins your calls. It only ever sees the meeting audio and screen already on your own machine, tied to your own sign-in.

  • Sign in with your own Google account — nothing shared across users.
  • Transcripts, attendees, and screenshots are stored under your account.
  • No bot joins your meeting — Teeline listens locally on your device.
